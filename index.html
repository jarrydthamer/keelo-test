What is KƎƎLO™?

KƎƎLO™ is an industry-, organisation- and compute-agnostic Agentic Executive Operating System with Director, Forge and persistent organisational state. Its compute-agnostic architecture evaluates policy and delegated authority before controlled effective execution. WRSaaS™ (Waste Recovery Software as a Service) is its waste-recovery operating model.

WRSaaS means Waste Recovery Software as a Service: a service model for detecting, verifying, governing, recovering, reducing and preventing systemic operational waste.

The KƎƎLO™ operating loop is: Detect → Verify → Govern → Act → Measure → Reduce → Prevent.
