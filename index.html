KƎƎLO™ / CATEGORY

The Executive Operating System

Human-Governed · Compute-Agnostic · Organisation-Neutral

KƎƎLO™ governs the relationship between authority, execution, evidence and outcome.

CAPABILITY ≠ AUTHORITY

The ability to propose or perform an action does not create the authority to execute it. Intelligence can recommend an action. KƎƎLO™ determines whether execution is authorised under organisational state, policy and delegated authority. Evidence establishes what may be known. Current authority establishes what may be done. KƎƎLO™ governs the transition from authorised intent to controlled outcome.

Evidence→Authority→Execution→Outcome→Reconciliation
